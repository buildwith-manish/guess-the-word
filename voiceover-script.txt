# Guess the Word by Emoji — Full Voiceover Script v4
**Channel:** Quiz Matrix | **Audience:** US | **50 Questions + 5 Bonus Rounds**

---

## How to Read This Script

Each question has **three parts**:

- **[QQ CLIP]** — Record as `qq##.mp3`. Plays after blanks appear, before timer starts. Give a sideways clue — a use case, feeling, or angle. Never describe the emojis. Under 5 seconds.
- **[SCREEN TEXT]** — On-screen text during the timer. NOT recorded. Max 8 words.
- **[REVEAL VO]** — Record as `r##.mp3`. Plays after the answer stamps in.
  - Non-fact questions → 2–3 words max. Punch and move.
  - Fact questions (⭐) → answer word + one sentence fact.

**Delivery style:** Upbeat, fast-paced, game-show energy. Punch the answer word hard on every reveal.
