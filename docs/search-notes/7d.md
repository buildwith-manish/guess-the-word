## Loudness note (by generator gain only)
Order is the oscillator/noise gain written in scripts/generate-audio.js, before the common factor `MASTER * sfxGain` that every sfx gets. It ignores length, pitch and envelope, and any per-cue `volume` set in the cue lists (I did not check those). No claim about how any of it sounds.

Highest gain, cues the search video uses:
- `thump` 0.55 (second beat 0.45): the clock's last three seconds, the level card stinger.
- `stamp` 0.38 low sine plus 0.10 on top at the same time (0.48 summed): every reveal.
- `riser` noise ramps up to about 0.32, plus a 0.12 tone: the last second of every clock.
- `jingle-rules` 0.2 (two notes): level card.
- `pop` 0.22: chips in the bonus, and the stand-in for "search landed" until the swap above.
- `title` 0.12, `remind` 0.12.

Lowest: `key-click` 0.035 and 0.05 (two layered voices), `tick` 0.07, `send` 0.09.

Order, loudest first: thump, stamp, riser, pop, jingle-rules, remind, send, tick, key-click.
`drumroll` (peaks 0.26) and `whoosh` (peaks 0.30, used on every iris) are noise built in separate blocks, so their gains are not directly comparable with the tone table. `whoosh` plays at every iris, so it may be worth a look next to the stamp.
