## Known issues in the 10 cells
- Gemini drew a white sticker border with a grey shadow. The cutter removes it (background = white OR pale colourless pixels connected to the image border), but a faint pale rim may remain on some edges (donut, flag). Checked by eye on a checkerboard preview only; not re-checked on a real background.
- White enclosed by the outline stays opaque on purpose (eyes, flag squares, donut hole), so the donut hole shows as a white disc.
- Sources are ~140-185 px wide, so the 512x512 output is soft.
