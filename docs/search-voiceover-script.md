# Voiceover script (US English)

Voice: warm, relaxed American, mid-tempo, a little under conversational speed. Smile slightly on the reveals, stay flat and calm on the clues. Leave a short beat before each reveal. Master at about -14 LUFS, voice clearly above the music.

Pronunciation notes: Pokémon = POH-kay-mahn. WALL-E = WALL-ee. Spider-Man, Scooby-Doo, SpongeBob SquarePants as written. Pikachu = PEEK-ah-choo. Moana = moh-AH-nah. Gollum = GAH-lum.

File names are a suggestion; each clip goes into `src/search/search-voiceover-manifest.json` as `{ "file": "...", "seconds": N }` under the key shown.

## Intro  (key: intro, file intro.mp3, about 6 s)

> Everyone's got a search history they'd never show anyone. Here are thirty characters, and all you get is what they typed. Guess who it is before the clock runs out.

## Level cards  (key: level 1-3, about 4 s each)

1. **EASY** (level-1.mp3, 5 seconds to answer)
> Level one, easy. Ten characters you already know. You'll have five seconds to answer.

2. **MEDIUM** (level-2.mp3, 5 seconds to answer)
> Level two, medium. The clues get a little sneakier. You'll have five seconds to answer.

3. **HARD** (level-3.mp3, 5 seconds to answer)
> Level three, hard. These are the ones that really make you think. Five seconds to answer. Good luck.

## Clues  (key: clue > round id > 0-3)

Each clue is read aloud exactly as it types on screen, one clue per search, hardest first. Read them flat and curious, like you're reading someone's phone over their shoulder. No lead-in words.

## Reveals  (key: reveal > round id)

Short and warm. One beat of silence before each.

## Scoreboard  (key: scoreboard > rank, about 5 s each)

Score is out of 30. Read the line for the rank the viewer sees.

- **legend** (27 or more, scoreboard-legend.mp3): That's a legend. Twenty-seven or more. You clearly know your characters.
- **expert** (21 or more, scoreboard-expert.mp3): Expert. Really impressive. Just a few slipped past you.
- **good** (15 or more, scoreboard-good.mp3): Good. More than half right. Not bad at all.
- **beginner** (9 or more, scoreboard-beginner.mp3): Beginner. You've got a solid start. The hard round probably got you.
- **keepgoing** (under 9, scoreboard-keepgoing.mp3): Keep going. Everybody starts somewhere. Try again and see how many you get.

## Notes

- Pause on a reveal is the one sound that should feel like a reward. Keep that clip clean, no music tail.
- Clues are the exact on-screen text from `src/search/search-data.ts`. If you change a search there, regenerate that clue.
- Reminder cards (after rounds 8 and 22) have no voiceover slot. If you want a line there, say so and I'll add a manifest key.
